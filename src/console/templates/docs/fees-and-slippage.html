Index options are bought on premium turnover. Charges are computed per executed order:

  • Brokerage: a flat amount per order (brokerage_per_order).
  • Exchange transaction charge: premium turnover × nse_txn_pct (NIFTY, NSE_FO) or bse_txn_pct (SENSEX, BSE_FO).
  • SEBI fee: turnover × sebi_per_crore / 1 crore.
  • STT: sell side only, turnover × stt_sell_pct.
  • Stamp duty: buy side only, turnover × stamp_buy_pct.
  • GST: gst_pct of brokerage + exchange + SEBI fees.

The current rates are on the Risk & safety page. They are configuration, not live data: verify them against your broker's contract notes, because exchanges and the government revise them.

Slippage

Slippage is the difference between the price you expected and the price you got. It is already inside every fill price, so net P&L includes it; the console's slippage column only estimates its size:

  • Paper: slippage_pct × (entry value + exit value).
  • Live: (entry fill − paper signal price) × quantity, when the paper trade for the same position exists.
  • Backtest: modelled inside the fills and not separately recorded.

Why costs matter

On short intraday option trades, charges plus slippage often take a large share of the gross result. The Performance page shows charges as a percentage of gross P&L so you can see when costs, not the signal, decide the outcome.
