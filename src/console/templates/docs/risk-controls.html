Risk limits live in [RISK] and [TRADE_PLAN] of config/scanner.ini and are enforced by the scanner on every entry. The console shows them and today's usage; it does not change them.

Per trade

  • Risk per trade is a percentage of capital. Size = floor(budget / risk per lot) whole lots; a trade is skipped when one lot risks too much.
  • The premium stop is capped at max_premium_loss_pct; entries need at least min_rr reward to risk after costs.

Per day and week

  • Daily loss limit (realised + open; the tighter of rupees and percent), weekly loss limit, trades per index and per session, open positions, losing-streak stop, same-direction and correlated exposure (NIFTY and SENSEX on the same side count as one bet), open premium and lots per expiry.
  • Session gates: no entries before the watch window or after last_entry, forced square-off, an expiry-day cut-off, a VIX ceiling, a gap filter and the event calendar.

Never

No martingale, no averaging down, no size increase after a loss. Sizing is only ever reduced to fit a limit.

Kill switch

A file named KILL_SWITCH in the project folder blocks new entries and squares off open positions within one cycle. Engage it from the Risk page (any user) or create the file by hand; releasing it from the console needs an admin and the word RELEASE.
