Every figure in the console comes from one of three sources. Each is labelled, carries its own rail, and is never combined with another.

Live (broker) real fills at Upstox. Read from data/execution.db (positions the order engine held at a non-paper broker, with their fills) and, on the Broker monitor page, straight from the Upstox API. Charges are computed from the fills at the [COSTS] rates; the broker's contract note is the final record. Slippage is the entry fill against the paper signal price of the same position.

Paper (simulated) the scanner's v1 / v2 / v3 books (data/prebreakout.db) and the movement and strategy books (data/movement.db). Fills are at the live ask + slippage and bid - slippage, net of charges. The slippage shown is the modelled percentage applied to both legs.

Backtest (modelled) files output/*_trades.csv written by the backtest commands. Option premiums are Black-Scholes values with IV = India VIX, a modelled spread and slippage. There are no historical option quotes, so liquidity filters and the derivatives module are untested historically.

What is excluded

  • Mock-mode records (synthetic data from --mock) are never shown in the console's results.
  • Open positions are not counted in closed-trade statistics; they are listed separately with their open P&L.

Nothing is estimated to fill a gap

If a source has no data, the page says so. Missing values are shown as a dash, never as zero and never as a sample figure.
