Feeds & services

Index scores are setup-strength scores (heuristic), not probabilities. WATCH = price near a level, not broken. CONFIRMED = a completed close beyond the level.
Stock plans use completed 15-minute bars. Stop-loss sits behind the latest swing / Supertrend (0.6–1.8 ATR); targets are 1.5 R and 2.5 R pulled in before the nearest resistance or support, and a plan is VALID only when the reward:risk still holds on the option premium after charges. Premium levels are delta-gamma estimates – the spot levels are the primary trigger.
Before the breakout: READY means the stock is coiled next to a level: place the trigger (buy-stop above for CE, sell-stop below for PE), do not enter early. A trade is taken (paper) only when a completed 15-minute bar closes beyond the level with a strong body and 1.5x volume after a READY on that level. Stop sits behind the base; after T1 the stop moves to cost.
Intraday cash and Swing (NIFTY 50 equity) default to paper trading; live orders need arming for the day and a confirmation per order. Their scores are heuristic, not win probabilities.
