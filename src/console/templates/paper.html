Paper trading

Simulated books that run inside the scanner (python -m src.main) and the movement service (python -m src.movement). No order reaches an exchange from these books.

How paper fills are simulated

Entry

Bought at the live ask plus {{ pb.slippage_pct }}% slippage; when no ask is quoted, LTP plus half of an assumed {{ pb.assumed_spread_pct }}% spread.

Exit

Sold at the live bid minus {{ pb.slippage_pct }}% slippage. Positions are never exited on a missing quote.

Charges

Brokerage, STT, exchange and SEBI fees, stamp duty and GST at the rates in [COSTS], on every leg.

Order mirror: lane {{ exec.lane or 'v1' }} is also sent through the full order path to the {{ 'simulated paper broker' if exec.broker == 'paper' or not exec.broker else exec.broker }} ({{ exec.mode or 'paper' }} mode), journalled in data/execution.db; see the order journal.
