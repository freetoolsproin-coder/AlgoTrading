Alerts

READY means the stock is coiled next to a level: place the trigger (buy-stop above for CE, sell-stop below for PE), do not enter early. A trade is taken (paper) only when a completed 15-minute bar closes beyond the level with a strong body and 1.5x volume after a READY on that level. Stop sits behind the base, T1 / T2 are 1.5 R / 2.5 R pulled in before the next level; after T1 the stop moves to cost. Option premiums are delta-gamma estimates at the trigger. Paper trading only; not investment advice.

Plans use completed 15-minute bars. Stop-loss sits behind the latest swing / Supertrend (0.6–1.8 ATR); targets are 1.5 R and 2.5 R pulled in before the nearest resistance or support, and a plan is VALID only when the reward:risk still holds on the option premium after charges. Premium levels are delta-gamma estimates – the spot levels are the primary trigger. Paper trading only; not investment advice.
