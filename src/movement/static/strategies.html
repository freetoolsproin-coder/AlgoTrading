Every strategy is its own paper book: an UP signal buys a CE, a DOWN signal buys a PE (simulated, no orders). Stops, targets and trailing stops are premium % from [STRATEGIES] / [STRATEGY_<KEY>] in config/scanner.ini. Every parameter is an untested starting value: judge each one by its own backtest and paper results (python -m src.movement.backtest --strategies KEY).
